A hull for the PCB I designed in week 1 of half life of hack club. 

This build is designed to be as aerodynamic as possible, as such it is designed after the shape of a football. It also need empty space to make it as heavy as possible, while small enough to fit inside the body of a competitive plane. It needs to survive a drop test and also have wire input from an external receiver from the fuselage.
